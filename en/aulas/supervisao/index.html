Lesson 7 · 20 min

Supervision and exceptions

With the matrix in place, Hora handles the routine on her own and sends Patrícia whatever is not routine. That creates a new job that no manager's job description had: the exception queue. In this lesson you work that queue in a simulation and leave with the daily and weekly routine of someone who supervises agents.

Start the lesson

In this lesson you will

  1. 1Separate normal, doubtful and critical cases
  2. 2Require the exception package from the agent
  3. 3Use the right verb: approve, send back or stop
  4. 4Build the supervision routine and measure its load

Goal: work the exception queue: approve, send back or stop each case with a reason, an owner and a deadline, in a daily and weekly routine.

    03 · The routine of whoever supervises

    The simulation is one late afternoon. These are the four pieces that make the queue work every day.

    1. Who resolves each type

    Insufficient or conflicting data goes back to whoever can complete it (the agent, with the customer, or Juliana). A request outside the policy goes to whoever has the authority limit (Patrícia, or Carlos above her). A critical case goes to a defined person and can halt the operation.

    The destination of each type is written in the constitution. An exception that needs a meeting to find out whose it is has already arrived late.

    At HorizonteMissing data → send back to the customer through Hora, with a deadline. Outside the policy → Patrícia (up to 10%) or Carlos. Manipulation or data leaking out → stop, Patrícia and Rafael.

    2. Demand the package

    An approval request without evidence shifts the work of investigating onto you. The agent brings the package: case, pending action with the exact content, reason, evidence, what it already tried, the rule involved and a recommendation.

    An incomplete package is sent back to the agent, not completed by you. Within two weeks, the packages arrive whole.

    Quick quizCan you decide by reading only the package, without opening the whole conversation? If not, the package is incomplete.

    3. Stopping is a button

    Stopping is different from sending back: there is risk, so the case (or the whole agent, on the off switch) leaves normal operation until someone understands what happened.

    The script fits in four lines: stop, contain (confirm through a known channel, undo what you can), log the incident and look for similar cases. Afterward, the cause becomes a test case.

    The hotel caseStop the order. Call the buyer at the phone number on file. Log the incident. Scan this week's conversations for similar text.

    4. Measure the supervision load

    The queue has a cost too: how long each case waits and how many minutes a day you spend deciding. If the queue grows, either the matrix is too tight or the agent is escalating without a package.

    These numbers feed the cost per result in lesson 2 and the decision to promote in lesson 5. Supervision that nobody measures becomes the manager's invisible overtime.

    At Horizonte (fictional numbers)First week: 9 exceptions a day, 40 minutes of Patrícia's time, average wait of 2 h. Third week: 3 a day, 15 minutes, wait of 40 minutes.

    05 · Practice

    Train on Patrícia's queue; then build your own routine and measure it for a week.

    1 · The simulated queue with the top score

    10 min

    Run this lesson's simulation. If you don't reach the top score, read the comments, start over and try again.

    Done when: you finished the simulation with the top score and can say, for each of the three cases, which verb you used and why.

    2 · Three justified decisions

    20 min

    Fill in an exception package with the decision for each case in the simulation: the hotel's order with the hidden instruction, Seu Antônio's 15% discount, and the customer without a CNPJ. In the justification, cite the rule from the matrix or the constitution that decided it.

    Done when: you have three packages, each with a different verb (stop, approve with a reason, send back), the rule cited, the next step with a deadline and a proposed learning.

    3 · Your routine, measured

    15 min + 1 week

    Copy the supervision routine and adapt it to your process: fixed time, backup and the items that make sense. Run the daily routine for a week, noting the number of exceptions, minutes spent and longest wait. Close the week with the 8 questions.

    Done when: the routine is written with an owner and a backup, 5 days are logged with exceptions, minutes and wait, and the weekly review answered the 8 questions with at least one decision.

    07 · Summary

    Three types of caseNormal follows the matrix; doubtful goes to the queue; critical goes first and can stop everything.
    Package before the decisionExact content, reason, evidence and recommendation. No package, send it back.
    Three verbsApprove with a reason, send back with a deadline, stop with containment and a record.
    Measured routineA short daily one, a weekly one with 8 questions, and supervision load as a number.
