Lesson 4 · 20 min

Authority limits: green, yellow, red

Every company has authority limits for people: how much discount a salesperson can give, the amount from which a manager signs. At Horizonte, Hora got unlimited authority: any discount, any amount, no record. In this lesson you decide, action by action, what the agent does on its own, what it prepares for you to approve, and what it passes along without touching.

Start the lesson

In this lesson you will

  1. 1Give authority limits by action, not by agent
  2. 2Choose the color by risk: amount, reversibility, effect outside the company
  3. 3Tie the approval to the exact content and a deadline
  4. 4Require a record of every action and an off switch

Goal: decide, action by action, what the agent does on its own, what it prepares for you to approve, and what it passes along, with an approver and a record.

01 · Three colors, five levels

Each action the agent takes falls into a color. The color comes from the risk, not from how much you like the agent. Tap each band.

Click each block in the diagram to see what it does.

02 · Four rules of authority limits

They fix three failures from the lab: F5 (discount with no cap), F7 (no record or approval above R$ 5 thousand) and F9 (email that sends and deletes without approval).

1. By action, by risk

For each action, ask: how much does it cost if it goes wrong? Can it be undone cheaply? Does it leave the company (customer, supplier, bank)? Does it touch personal data? Three easy answers: green. Any heavy one: yellow. Irreversible or expensive: red.

A rule with no exceptions: nothing irreversible in green, no matter how routine it looks.

F5 fixed (fictional policy)Discount up to 5%: green. From 5% to 10%: yellow, Patrícia approves. Above 10%: red, Hora does not offer it; Patrícia takes it to Carlos if she wants.

2. An approval has content and a deadline

Patrícia approves the hotel's monthly list: R$ 28,120 at list price. An hour later, the customer adds 20 boxes of laundry detergent. The earlier approval does not cover the new order.

An approval holds for the exact content, in that version, until a deadline. Without that, a "yes" in the morning authorizes anything in the afternoon.

F7 fixedOrder above R$ 5 thousand: yellow. Hora prepares it, Patrícia approves that order within 2 business hours; any change requires a new approval. Only then does it go to picking.

3. Least privilege, its own identity

Horizonte's email assistant was connected with permission to read, send, delete and empty the trash, all without approval, after a "go ahead and connect it" from Carlos over WhatsApp. Nobody decided the authority limit: it came as the tool's default.

The agent gets its own named access, only to what the responsibility requires. Okta (AI Agents at Work 2026, a survey by an identity vendor) reports that almost two-thirds of organizations apply weaker security controls to agents than to employees.

F9 fixedReading the sales inbox: green. Sending a reply: yellow. Deleting an email and emptying the trash: red, out of the agent's reach.

4. Log and off switch

Hora's setup turned off the action log "to save database space." Result: finance asked who approved the hotel order and nobody knew.

Every action the agent takes is logged: who, when, what, how much and with which version. And there is an off switch: who can press it, how, and tested at least once.

At HorizonteLog on for every action. Off switch: Patrícia or Rafael pause Hora in the WhatsApp dashboard; Juliana takes over the conversations. Tested on a Monday morning.

04 · Practice

Hora's matrix fixes two failures from the lab; yours applies to your agent.

1 · Hora's matrix

30 min

Open Hora's setup in the lab and list everything its tools allow. Build the matrix with the template, one line per action. Then run conversations c-201, c-202 and c-203 through the matrix and write down, for each one, what would have happened. Bonus: the email assistant's lines.

Done when: the matrix has at least 8 actions, none irreversible in green, discount in three ranges with numbers, the line for orders above R$ 5 thousand with approver and deadline, a log on every line, the off switch with names, and all three conversations run through the matrix.

2 · Your agent's matrix

30 min

Build the matrix for the agent in your job sheet. Ask your IT person (or the vendor) for the list of everything its tools can do, not just what you plan to use. Run the forgotten-action prompt and decide on every point it raises.

Done when: the matrix is saved with every action the tools allow, each with color, approver, owner and reversal, and you can say who presses the off switch and who takes over the work.

Lab pieces: WhatsApp agent (F5, F7) and email assistant (F9).
